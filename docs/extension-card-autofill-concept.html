01 / TRIGGER

Only on payment fields

The VELA icon appears in recognized card fields. No card list appears on ordinary text inputs.

02 / IDENTIFY

Masked by default

Show a nickname, card type, last four digits, and expiry. The full number stays out of the chooser.

03 / COMPLETE

Fill, then review

Selecting a card fills supported fields and closes the chooser. The user reviews the form and presses Pay themselves.
