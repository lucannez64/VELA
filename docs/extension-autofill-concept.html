01 / DISCOVER

Small field icon

A quiet VELA button sits inside supported fields. Clicking it opens the chooser without covering the whole page.

02 / CHOOSE

Matched accounts only

Names and usernames distinguish identities. The current website is visible in the chooser header.

03 / FILL

One deliberate action

Click an account or use arrow keys and Enter. The chooser closes after filling; it does not submit the form.
