## Proof-level mutation checks

Both mutate a **copy** of `proofs/fstar/extraction-login-driver/Vela_session_policy.Challenge.fst`
in `/tmp/opencode`; the production source and the checked extraction are never
patched. Each run is the focused F* invocation above restricted to the
Challenge module and the two challenge lemma modules, with
`--include /tmp/opencode/mutant-<name>` replacing
`--include proofs/fstar/extraction-login-driver`, and `--z3rlimit 8000`
(everything else identical, including `--z3version 4.13.3`).

Truncation mutant (`mutant-truncation-length.diff`): the retained length is
truncated at nine and the Rust `ensures` is weakened to `Prims.l_True`, so the
truncation is not caught by the extracted contract. The independent producer
lemma must catch it. Command:
